## What we do

- **Field sales & distribution management** — run large field teams across regions
- **Campaign & sales tracking** — plan campaigns and track sales in real time
- **Reporting & dashboards** — operational reports for managers and head office
- **Android apps for field teams** — mobile tools that work where the sales happen
- **Training platforms** — onboard and upskill field staff at scale
